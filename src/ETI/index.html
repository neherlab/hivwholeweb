Estimate time since infection from NGS diversity

Introduction
This page implements the method to estimate the date of infection from NGS diversity data as presented in Puller et al, biorxiv, 10.1101/129387.
The estimates are based on whole genome diversity data from the eleven patients studied here with well characterized date of infection. Those data are used to estimate the rate at which intra-host diversity increases over time, which in turn is used to estimate the date of infection from diversity measurements.
